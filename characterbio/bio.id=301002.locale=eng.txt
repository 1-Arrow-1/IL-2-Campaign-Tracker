<p>From the time he was young, Frenchman $[name] always sought out adventure and loved sightseeing. His parents also enjoyed travelling. While he was young, his family went on trips all around Europe - Moscow, Istanbul, London, Berlin, Paris, Rome, Venice, and so on. $[firstName] had quite the childhood. He was always at the top of his class in school and by the time he was 17, he was one of the smartest young men in his hometown of Orleans.</p>
<p>Time went on and for a while $[firstName] travelled with his young girlfriend. Together they travelled farther than ever before to places such as New York, Gibraltar, and Nassau, as he was not a poor man. His father was coincidentally an aeroplane designer, having had much to do with the design of the Nieuport II, III, and IV. The French government paid him generously for this work. $[firstName] thus was accustomed to money, adventure, and flight. Though he never directly showed any interest in aeroplanes, he had a slight curiosity in them, due mainly to his father's work.</p>
<p>War erupted in 1914, but $[lastName] was no longer living in France. He and his lover had moved to Antigua in the West Indies some time before the war began. When he heard of the war, he caught the first boat to France and enlisted in the Aeronautique Militaire. He joined largely out of instinct. His father flew, and he thought he should as well. $[lastName] found that he loved flying and was quoted as saying, "In all my life of travelling and exploring, I never thought of doing it from the air. It's a different world above the clouds."</p>

He had loved to see his beloved French countryside from the air until the first day he caught a glimpse of the front. His view on flying changed. He learned that he must fight and possibly die for his country so that his family, friends, and fellow Frenchmen may live.</p>
<p>$[lastName] hopes that when the war ends, he can move back to Antigua and marry his long-time girlfriend. He knows in the back of his mind, however, that he may never come home.
