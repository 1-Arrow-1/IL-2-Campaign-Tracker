<p>I was born on $[birthDate] in the town of Beeskow, the land of Brandenburg, that is at the exit of the Spree from the Schwielochsee, to the family of a small merchant, and I was the fourth and the youngest child in the family. My older brother and two sisters were much older than me, and for them I was always like a doll, and with me everyone was fussing and paying too much attention. "Oh, our little $[firstName]," they always said when I did something.</p>
<p>We did not live very lavishly - my mother worked as a nurse in a local municipal hospital, and my father owned a shop selling hardware. The elder brother, having finished school, got a job as an accountant assistant, becoming the most successful child in our family. My father was very proud of him and would point to him as an example every time when I brought bad marks from school, calling me an obscure bugger.</p>
<p>In the mid-thirties, with money I had set aside, I secretly visited the local ballooning and modeling aircraft club, and then, having distinguished myself in this field, I was sent to the school of gliding with the support of the German Air Sports Association. You should have seen the face of my father, when a paper from the club came in my name - he kept saying that "our unlucky little $[firstName] wants to break his neck!" But I maintained my insistence, which puzzled my father, but he eventually relented. My sisters were so happy that, escorting me, they arranged a small party. The elder Elsa was very proud of me, as it emphasized her status as well, because she began to associate with a Wehrmacht officer candidate, whom she met not so long ago. "And you will become an officer!," she repeated, although at that time I did not think about it, because my main goal was to fly airplanes.</p>
<p>In September 1939, war broke out in Europe, and I began to fear that I would not have time to finish my studies and take part in the hostilities. But the conflict was only growing, and now, on $[startDate] I, in the rank of $[startRank], was sent to the airfield of $[startSquadronName]. I still have the opportunity to show what I really can do.</p>
