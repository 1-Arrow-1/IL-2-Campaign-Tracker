<p>Born at Mossel Bay in $[birthDate], $[name] was attending the University of Cape Town when the War broke out. Subsequently he enlisted and served as a signaller in the Duke of Edinburgh's Own Rifles in German South-West Africa. Transferred to the South African Field Telegraph and Postal Corps, he was demobilised in August 1915 and returned to his studies.</p>
<p>$[firstName] was discouraged to re-enlist on account of his short stature (1.57m), but he was recruited into the Central Flying School by an ex-boy of his school. He left for England and undertook a short pilot training. Because of his height, special adjustments have always had to be made to the rudder bar and seat of his aircraft. Despite these difficulties and a crash during training, $[lastName] persisted, and so he was posted to $[startSquadronName] on $[startDate] as a $[startRank].</p>
