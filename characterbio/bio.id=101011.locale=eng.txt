Until the German Blitzkrieg was unleashed on $[firstName]'s homeland he did not truly exercise patriotism. Sure, he had done his part for the good of the people but it was his job, not his duty. The invasion changed that in a hurry. Anything left behind that the Germans could put to use was destroyed or burned to the ground.
