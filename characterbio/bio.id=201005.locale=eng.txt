<p>Medals are all that matters. That's what I say!</p>
<p>Let the old men and the politicians rant and rave over their Lebensraum and their German destiny. What does any of that matter to me? The future belongs to the young. When this war is fought and won; those with the Knight's Cross around their necks will be the only ones that matter.</p>
<p>A man with medals on his chest and around his throat will be the elite in any company. A smart uniform and my fair share of decorations will open doors that would otherwise be closed to me. My words will carry weight. All my wishes will be granted - whether at work, at play, or in love... After all, who can deny a hero of anything?</p>
<p>My training is done and I'm off to Russia any day now, to join $[startSquadronName]. I'm ready too. I know I am. My instructor said I'm as good as anybody. Certainly as good as any Ivan, I'll wager. Just point me at them, and I'll show you how it's done!</p>
<p>I must admit, though - when I received the news of my posting abroad, I was disappointed not to be going to France. They say it is like a rest cure there. A holiday. A good clean fight with the Tommies during the day, and then all the comforts of home and more besides at night. You can get anything in France. Anything at all, and it's yours for the taking. Just walk into any store. Just grab what you like and send it home - and it's all on credit!</p>
<p>Not at all like in Russia. Definitely not like in Russia. Just one scruffy little village after another they say, and no nightlife to speak of. They even have lice. Lice. Just think of it! Still, it is not all bad. They say the victories come quick and easy in the East. If you want rapid promotion and some fast recognition, Russia is the place to be.</p>
<p>So there's the plan. A quick stint of service in the East. Just enough to make a name for myself. Six months. Maybe a year at the most if the war lasts that long. I'll get some easy victories and play the newsreel cameras and news photographers like a fiddle. Get some eye-catching markings on my machine perhaps - something they won't be able to resist showing in the cinemas back home. Old-man Richthofen had his little red triplane, so why not something for me?</p>
<p>Once the medals then start flowing in, I can get a transfer home and back out of that dung heap. My father is an influential man after all. He knows the Herr Doktor, and he knows the value of a hero for a son. Maybe I can even get a spot in one of those home defense units. Somewhere handy to Berlin... Oh yes, that's the way to do it!</p>
<p>Mark my words - <i>Medals are all that matter...</i></p>
