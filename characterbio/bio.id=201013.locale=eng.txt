<p>Born an only child on $[birthDate] in Merida (Extremadura), the former Roman capital of Lusitania, a rural region southwest of Spain bordered by Portugal, $[name] grew up tough. With a German mother and despite having a civil servant teacher father, his restless nature made him prone to play imaginary wars in the fields with the other town kids. On those fields is where he first saw an aeroplane passing over his head and since then, his dream was to be able one day to fly on one of those incredible machines. In 1929, in a last-ditch attempt to tame him, $[firstName] was sent to a religious boarding school in Salamanca to prepare him for university. He managed to pass his entrance examinations and started a History and Geography degree in Ancient History, but inevitably, the Spanish Civil War broke out on June 18, 1936 and he saw it as his chance to seek hair-raising adventure. He had previously joined the Falange Española union party at university and, almost immediately, enlisted in the Nationalist armies.</p>
<p>After half a year of fighting in infantry, due to his excellent physical condition, flawless eyesight and considering the need to train new pilots, his superiors accepted his request for a transfer to the small Spanish Air Force. $[firstName] was sent to the aerodrome of Tablada, Sevilla. He began training with other young pilots to get his wings. He soon excelled in gunnery and acrobatics. It did not take too much time for García Morato, the leading Nationalist fighter ace, to see that this young cadet's passion for flying and aggressiveness made him be one of the very few naturals.</p>
<p>In September 1937 he was appointed to join 2-G-3 Fighter Group commanded by Angel Salas Larrazabal. They flew the Italian Fiat Cr.32 'Chirri' and during 1938 Alferez $[lastName] took part in all major air battles drawing blood and confirming his first aerial victories.</p>
<p>In June 1941 Germany attacked the Soviet Union. Spain decided to return the favor to Germany for her help during the Spanish Civil War and sent a division of volunteers, 'the Blue Division', and a squadron of pilots. Strong in his Falangist beliefs to support Germany's crusade against Communism, $[startRank] $[lastName], thanks to his double nationality, only had to make a choice between joining the Luftwaffe or accepting Salas' invitation to join his brothers-in-arms in the first 'Blue Squadron'. After a brief training period in Werneuchen, near Berlin, $[firstName] finally make the decision of join the $[startSquadronName] on $[startDate].</p>
<p>For the second time in his life, this young idealist embarked on his endless quest for glory hoping it would not be his last …</p>
